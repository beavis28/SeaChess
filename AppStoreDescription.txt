SeaChess - Strategic Board Game for Apple Watch

Dive into the depths of strategic gameplay with SeaChess, a captivating 4x4 board game designed exclusively for Apple Watch. Battle against an intelligent AI opponent in this simplified chess-like game featuring sea creatures.

GAME RULES:

BOARD & SETUP
• Play on a 4x4 grid
• Your pieces start randomly placed on the bottom row
• AI pieces start randomly placed on the top row
• Each player has 4 pieces: Whale, Octopus, Crab, and Small Fish

PIECES & MOVEMENT
🐋 Whale (King): Moves 1 square in any direction (8 directions)
🐙 Octopus (Rook): Moves 1 square horizontally or vertically (4 directions)
🦀 Crab (Bishop): Moves 1 square diagonally (4 directions)
🐟 Small Fish (Pawn): Moves 1 square forward only
🦈 Shark (Promoted): When Small Fish reaches opponent's back row, it promotes to Shark and can move forward, backward, sideways, and diagonally forward

CAPTURING & HAND PIECES
• When you capture an opponent's piece, it goes to your hand
• You can drop captured pieces on any empty square on your turn
• Small Fish cannot be dropped on the opponent's back row
• If you capture a Shark, it becomes a Small Fish in your hand

HOW TO WIN
• Capture the opponent's Whale

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a piece to select it - green squares show valid moves
• Tap a green square to move your piece
• Tap a piece in your hand, then tap an empty square to drop it
• Opponent pieces are shown upside down for easy identification

FEATURES:
• Intuitive touch controls optimized for Apple Watch
• Smart AI opponent with adjustable difficulty
• Beautiful sea creature themed pieces
• Random starting positions for varied gameplay
• Hand piece management system
• Automatic piece promotion

Perfect for quick strategic sessions on your wrist. Challenge yourself against the AI and master the art of SeaChess!
